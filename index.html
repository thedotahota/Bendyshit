Bendmarks

Bend marks and cut length for tube with 90° bends. All lengths in mm.

User

Pipe

+x runs left to right, +y away from you, +z downward. Give a letter and a length, like y200 (add − for the opposite direction). Two numbers after one letter, like x490 45, mean 490 minus 45, so a 445 mm leg in the + direction.

Drawing

Green dot is the tube start (origin), red dot is the end, numbered circles are the bends (filled = bought elbow). In every view +x runs left to right, +y points away from you and +z points down.
